Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Color Connect ("we", "the game", or "the app") handles information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe gaming experience.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Color Connect is built with a "Privacy by Design" approach. We do not collect, store, or share any user data. Specifically:

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
  • No Tracking/Analytics: We do not use third-party analytics tools (like Firebase or Google Analytics) to track your behavior.
  • No Advertising: The app does not contain advertisements and does not access your Advertising ID (AAID).
  • No In-App Purchases: There are no financial transactions within the app, so no payment data is ever requested.

2. Device Permissions

The app does not require any special permissions to function. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, or location. All gameplay data (such as high scores or levels) is stored locally on your device and never leaves it.

3. Children's Privacy

Since we do not collect any data, Color Connect is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). It is safe for players of all ages.

4. Data Safety Declaration (Google Play)

In accordance with Google Play's Data Safety requirements, we declare that:

  • No user data is collected by this application.
  • No user data is shared with third parties.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
